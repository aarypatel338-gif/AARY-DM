How these numbers were made

Rules come from the 9 & 15 EMA scalping video: EMAs stacked and sloped at 30° or more, a pin bar, full-body or engulfing candle off the EMAs, stop at the signal candle, target at 1:2. Intraday 1m and 3m runs cover about 7 sessions, 5m to 30m about 60 days, and 1H and above about 2 years of Yahoo data. Option P&L uses estimated premiums and includes costs. Nifty uses a 65-unit lot and Sensex a 20-unit lot. All figures come from your EMA_Indicator_Backtest_Dashboard workbook.
